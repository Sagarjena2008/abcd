Learn & improve

Workout, nutrition & time management

Use this as general education, not as a personalised medical or diet plan. Ask a qualified trainer for exercise technique.

Beginner workout structure

  • Warm up: 5–10 minutes of easy movement and dynamic mobility.
  • Strength: Learn a few basic movements with light resistance and good form.
  • Cardio: Choose walking, cycling, or another activity suited to your ability.
  • Cool down: Slow down gradually and stretch gently if comfortable.
  • Recovery: Schedule rest days and increase activity gradually.

Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath; seek appropriate help.

Food, protein & hydration

  • Eat regular balanced meals with protein foods such as lentils, beans, dairy, eggs, tofu, fish, or meat if suitable for you.
  • Include carbohydrates, healthy fats, fruit, and vegetables to support overall energy and health.
  • Drink water regularly; needs vary with heat, activity, and individual health.
  • Protein powder is optional—not essential. Food can meet many people's needs.
  • Ask a registered dietitian or healthcare professional for individual advice, especially if you have a health condition.

This site does not prescribe supplements, doses, or personalised nutrition targets.
